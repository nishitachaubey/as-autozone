Which one suits you?

Tempos and cargo

Small trucks and vans that carry roughly half a tonne to a tonne. Common with shops, markets and delivery fleets.

Pickups

Good to know

Questions

What happens after I send my details?

Our team gets your enquiry and calls the number you gave. We discuss the vehicle, the current price and what you need.

Why are prices not shown?

Prices change by variant, city and offers. We share a current quote when we call, so you get the right figure for your vehicle.

Can I see a vehicle before buying?

Mention it in your enquiry and we will tell you what is possible for that model.

I am buying more than one vehicle. Can I enquire for a fleet?

Yes. Choose "Fleet or multiple vehicles" as the use and write how many you need in the message box.

Are the specifications on this page final?

They are indicative. Exact variants, payload, seating and features differ, so confirm them with our team before you decide.
